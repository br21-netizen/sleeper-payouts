🔗 Sleeper Data & Settings

Roster mapping and weekly points

Names are automatically matched when the available Sleeper names are unambiguous. For chopped teams without identifiable roster metadata, select historical roster IDs for chopped teams even if they no longer appear in Sleeper's current rosters. Mapping is saved on this device. No match-quality indicators are shown. The points table uses actual matchup points keyed by permanent roster ID, not projected points. Blank means data not loaded or unavailable.

Elimination weeks

Set the confirmed week each team was chopped. The last week with players and the first later empty-roster week are shown as an inferred cut boundary, not an officially confirmed elimination week.

Recover missing chopped-team scores

Sleeper can return chopped rosters with zero points and empty players. Those are valid post-elimination scores. The app stores all roster IDs and weekly records, including zeros. Paste a raw weekly matchup JSON response from Sleeper below, or enter a missing score manually. Both are saved on this device and retained after refreshing. Existing scores are never erased by a new sync.
